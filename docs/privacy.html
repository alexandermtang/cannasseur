Privacy Policy for cannasseur

Last updated: August 24, 2026

cannasseur ("the app") is a personal log for tracking your own cannabis use. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

The app collects only what's needed to run your account and store your logs:

  • Account information: your email address, used to sign in and manage your account.
  • Profile information: a display name, if you choose to set one.
  • Log entries: the data you enter when logging a session — strain name, product type, tags, mood and medical-effect ratings, notes, and the date.

We do not collect location data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

How We Use Your Information

Your account information and log entries are used solely to provide the app's core functionality — letting you record, search, filter, and review your own logs. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Data Storage

Your data is stored with Supabase, our backend provider, and transmitted using encrypted (TLS) connections. Only you can access your own logs — other users cannot see your entries.

Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except as required to operate the app (our backend infrastructure provider, Supabase) or if required by law.

Your Choices

You can edit or delete individual log entries at any time within the app. To request deletion of your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

cannasseur is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the legal age for cannabis use in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support@cannasseur.app.
